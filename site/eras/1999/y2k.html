Halcyon Airways and the Year 2000

Many computer systems store years as two digits, so on January 1, 2000 they may read "00" as 1900. We have taken this problem very seriously.

Since 1997 our engineers have checked every system that stores a date: reservations, ticketing, crew schedules, maintenance records and the clocks in our aircraft. Every one of them has been tested with dates after December 31, 1999.

What this means for you: a flight booked for January 1, 2000 will leave on January 1, 2000. Your Halcyon Club miles will not expire in 1900.

Halcyon will operate a normal schedule on New Year's Eve. Our chairman will be on the first flight of the year.

Last reviewed 03/14/99. This statement is a Year 2000 Readiness Disclosure under the Year 2000 Information and Readiness Disclosure Act.
